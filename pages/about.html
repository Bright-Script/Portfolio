About

The person behind the pixels.

I'm Brighton — a front-end developer who cares as much about how a site feels to use as how it looks in a screenshot.

Quick facts

  • Based in: Nairobi, Kenya
  • Focus: Front-end development, UI systems
  • Currently: Open to freelance projects
  • Outside of code: Design, photography, coffee

I got into web development by rebuilding my own portfolio over and over until I understood why the good ones felt good. That habit never really stopped — every project is still a chance to get one detail closer to right, whether that's a transition curve, a line height, or how a form explains what went wrong.

These days I split my time between client front-end work and small products of my own, usually somewhere in the overlap of design and code.
